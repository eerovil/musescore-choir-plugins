# Fixture: Virta venhettä vie

A real, complete song for **prototyping** the workflow end to end. Not a unit-test
fixture — nothing in `src/*/tests/` reads it, and it is free to change shape as the
app does. The point is to have one song that can be dropped into `songs/` at a known
stage, in seconds, so a new feature meets real OCR damage on the first run.

```bash
fixtures/virta-venhetta-vie/reset.sh        # drop it into songs/ at the furthest stage
fixtures/virta-venhetta-vie/reset.sh 00     # or: just registered, ready to clean
./song.py                                   # and open it
```

Working on it is destructive by design — `reset.sh` wipes `songs/virta-venhetta-vie`
and lays it down again. Nothing you do in the app can damage the fixture itself.

## The music

*Virta venhettä vie* (1906), music **Toivo Kuula** (1883–1918), words **Eino Leino**
(1878–1926), for TTBB male choir. Both authors died well over 70 years ago, so the
composition and the text are in the public domain. The committed PDF is a scan of
p. 118–121 of a songbook; the work is PD, but if that particular printing turns out
to be a recent reprint, its typographical arrangement could carry a separate
short-term right in some jurisdictions. Swap the scan if that ever matters — nothing
here depends on which edition it is.

## Why this song

It is ordinary enough to be representative and broken enough to be useful:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Shape** | 2 OCR staves (Tenor, Bass) x 2 voices, splitting to T1/T2/B1/B2 |
| **Size** | 52 measures, 15 printed systems, 4 pages |
| **Mode** | normal (not `--per-system`; staves keep their roles throughout) |
| **OCR** | Soundslice MusicXML export — no lyrics at all, so the lyric stage always runs from scratch |

What the pipeline actually hits on the way through:

- **reversed stem voices** in m1 on both tenor staves
- **genuine meter changes** — 4/4 → 2/4 (m10) → 4/4 (m13) → 3/4 (m27) → 2/4 (m28)
  → 4/4 (m29), all correctly judged genuine by `fix_spurious_timesigs`
- **triplets** in the *agitato* section
- **an unfixable corrupted measure** — m26 arrives with `len="9/8"`;
  `preprocess_corrupted_measures` logs "not possible to fix" and it survives as the
  one open health issue, `malformed-m26-s1-v0`
- **imitative writing with four independent lyric lines** (see STEPS.md step 3) —
  the above/below divisi routing gets a real workout, not a synthetic one
- **melismas whose slurs the OCR dropped** — the residual lyric mismatches

**Not** covered: per-system mode (`laulun_aika.mscx` stays the fixture for that), the
>2-voice interactive re-voicing path, and `--add`.

## Stages

Each stage is an **overlay** holding only the files it adds or changes, so the 745 KB
scan and the OCR XML are stored once. `reset.sh` layers them in order.

| | contents | state |
|---|---|---|
| `00-registered/` | PDF, OCR XML, `.song.json` | stage `clean` |
| `10-cleaned/` | + converted `.mscx`, `_cleaned.mscx` | stage `fix`, 1 open health issue |
| `20-lyrics/` | + `lyrics.json`, lyrics imported into `_cleaned.mscx` | stage `fix`, 12 lyric mismatches |

Everything after `00-registered/` is regenerated by:

```bash
.venv/bin/python fixtures/virta-venhetta-vie/build.py
```

which replays exactly what `server.py` does for the clean and lyrics stages, so the
snapshots are real app states rather than hand-made ones. It needs
`MUSESCORE_CLI_PATH`. `lyrics.json` is the one thing it does not generate — that is a
committed artifact; STEPS.md step 3 records where it came from.

The song never reaches `review`: `set_stage("review")` is gated on a mismatch-free
lyric import, and 12 mismatches remain. That is deliberate — see STEPS.md.

## Recording and upload

Out of scope. Those stages need macOS, MuseScore, QuickRecorder and OBS, and produce
media far too large to commit. `media/` is excluded from every snapshot.
